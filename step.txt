CREDITLEND — DEMO CLICK SCRIPT (actions only, no narration)
===========================================================
App: http://localhost:3210   ·   MetaMask on Sepolia
Writes → MetaMask Confirm (gas).   Decrypt → MetaMask Sign (no gas).

STEP 0 — Landing
- Open http://localhost:3210
- Click "Launch app →"

STEP 1 — Connect
- Click "Connect Wallet" → approve in MetaMask
- Check network reads "sepolia"

STEP 2 — Faucet
- Left menu → "Faucet"
- On cEUR click "Mint 100,000" → Confirm
- On cUSDC click "Mint 100,000" → Confirm
- Click "Decrypt balance" on a row → Sign → balance shows

STEP 3 — Dashboard: submit + score
- Left menu → "Dashboard"
- Aggregate balance = 500000000
- Account age = 1000
- Click "Submit & score" → Confirm → wait for tx
- Click "Decrypt my score" → Sign
- Score + Risk band + breakdown appear

STEP 4 — Markets
- Left menu → "Markets"
- (just show the grid)

STEP 5 — Borrow
- Left menu → "Borrow"
- Select market "cEUR → cUSDC"
- Deposit collateral = 1000 → click "Deposit cEUR" → Confirm (approve/operator popup first if asked) → wait
- Borrow = 500 → click "Borrow cUSDC" → Confirm

STEP 6 — Positions
- Left menu → "Positions"
- Click "Decrypt positions" → Sign
- cEUR→cUSDC row shows collateral + debt (others show 0)

STEP 7 — Repay
- On Positions, cEUR→cUSDC row: type 500 → click "Repay"
- Confirm approve/operator popup → then Confirm Repay

STEP 8 — Credit line
- Left menu → "Credit line"
- Click "Decrypt my credit line" → Sign
- Reputation + credit line appear

STEP 9 — Guarantor
- Left menu → "Guarantor"
- Pick a Market
- Paste a Borrower address
- Enter a Backing amount
- Click the guarantee button → Confirm

STEP 10 — Liquidations
- Left menu → "Liquidations"
- Trigger card: pick market, paste borrower address → "Request" → Confirm
- Fulfill card: enter Request id → "Fulfill" → Confirm
- Auction card: enter Auction id + bid 900 → "Place bid" → Confirm
- Then "Settle + pay" → Confirm

STEP 11 — Compliance
- Left menu → "Compliance"
- Step 1: paste auditor address → "Set auditor" → Confirm
- Step 2: pick cEUR→cUSDC, paste same auditor address → "Grant scoped access" → Confirm
- Step 3: pick market + your borrower address → "Decrypt debt" → Sign

STEP 12 — Attack demo
- Left menu → "Attack demo"
- (show the page / run the demo action on it)
