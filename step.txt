CREDITLEND — FULL DEMO SCRIPT (every page, click along one step at a time)
==========================================================================
App: http://localhost:3210   ·   Network: Sepolia
Have MetaMask on Sepolia. If it errors on gas, set MetaMask's Sepolia RPC to:
  https://ethereum-sepolia-rpc.publicnode.com
Writes ("Submit & score", "Deposit", "Borrow", "Repay", "Bid"...) pop a MetaMask
CONFIRM (costs gas) — click Confirm and wait for the tx.
"Decrypt" buttons pop a MetaMask SIGNATURE (no gas) — just Sign.

The story: "Onchain lending is public — so credit can't exist onchain. CreditLend encrypts
creditworthiness itself, so you can borrow on reputation, below 100% collateral."

Demo order:
  0 Landing → 1 Connect → 2 Faucet → 3 Dashboard: SUBMIT + SCORE → 4 Markets →
  5 Borrow → 6 Positions → 7 Repay → 8 Credit line (reputation) → 9 Guarantor →
  10 Liquidations → 11 Compliance → 12 Attack demo → Closer

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 0 — Landing
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Open http://localhost:3210
- One line: "Every position onchain is a public broadcast. We encrypt the credit layer."
- Click "Launch app →".

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — Connect
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Click "Connect Wallet", approve in MetaMask.
- Confirm the network reads "sepolia".

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — Faucet (encrypted test tokens)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Faucet".
- On cEUR click "Mint 100,000". Confirm in MetaMask.
- (Optional) also mint cUSDC — the debt asset in the demo market.
- Say: "Balances are ERC-7984 ciphertexts — the chain never sees the amount."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — Dashboard: SUBMIT INPUTS → GET YOUR SCORE  ← the core privacy moment
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Dashboard".
- LEFT card "Submit encrypted inputs":
    · Aggregate balance (proof-of-funds) = leave 500000000 (or type your own)
    · Account age (pre-bucketed)         = leave 1000
    · Click "Submit & score".
    · MetaMask pops TWICE-ish: it encrypts in the browser, then Confirm the tx. Wait for it.
- Say: "These are encrypted in MY browser. The oracle computes my score + risk band ON
  CIPHERTEXT — the raw balance and age never touch the chain."
- RIGHT card "Your private credit report": after the tx confirms, click "Decrypt my score"
  → Sign in MetaMask.
- The report appears: the 3 bars (Balance / Account age / History), the Score, and the
  Risk band X/5 with its label (e.g. "Medium").
- Say: "This score is an euint32 onchain. Only I can decrypt it — nobody can profile me."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — Markets (6 isolated markets, live Chainlink)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Markets".
- Show the grid: each card = collateral→debt, a live Chainlink price, its feed, and LLTV.
- Say: "Six isolated markets. Prices are public Chainlink feeds; the collateral and debt tokens
  are ERC-7984 confidential. Public prices, private positions."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 — Borrow (deposit + borrow, all encrypted)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Borrow".
- In the market grid select "cEUR → cUSDC" (the undercollateralized demo market).
- Deposit collateral field = 1000 → click "Deposit cEUR". Confirm in MetaMask (approve/operator
  popup first if it asks), wait for the tx.
- Borrow field = 500 → click "Borrow cUSDC". Confirm in MetaMask.
- Say: "The amount is encrypted client-side. The pool picks my ratio from my ENCRYPTED band
  (from Step 3) and values collateral via Chainlink — all on ciphertext. Under-collateralized
  clamps to 0, it never reverts and leaks my balance."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 — Positions (only YOU can read your debt)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Positions".
- Click "Decrypt positions" → Sign in MetaMask.
- The cEUR→cUSDC row now shows your collateral + debt in plaintext — to you only.
  (Other rows stay "…" because you have no position there — that's expected.)
- Say: "The same handles are gibberish to everyone else on the explorer."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7 — Repay (build reputation)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Still on "Positions", cEUR→cUSDC row: type a repay amount (e.g. 500) → click "Repay".
- Confirm the operator/approve popup first, then the Repay tx.
- Say: "Each on-time repayment raises my ENCRYPTED reputation (+50); a miss decays it."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8 — Credit line → UNDERCOLLATERALIZED borrow (killer feature)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Credit line".
- Click "Decrypt my credit line" → Sign in MetaMask.
- Your reputation shows, and the credit line = reputation × 10 (higher after Step 7's repay).
- Say: "That line is added to my backing as VIRTUAL collateral. With enough reputation I can
  borrow BELOW 100% collateral — impossible on a transparent chain, where a public score gets
  gamed and front-run. Under FHE it just works."
- (Optional) go back to Borrow and borrow more than collateral alone would allow, to show it live.

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 9 — Guarantor (confidential third-party backing)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Guarantor".
- Pick a Market, paste a Borrower address, enter a Backing amount → click the guarantee button.
  Confirm in MetaMask.
- Say: "A third party posts ENCRYPTED backing to lift a borrower's limit — without revealing who
  backed whom, or for how much."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 10 — Liquidations (reveal-one-bit + sealed-bid auction)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Liquidations".
- Trigger card: pick the market, paste a borrower address → "Request". Confirm in MetaMask.
- Fulfill card: enter the Request id → "Fulfill". (Reveals ONLY the health bit.)
- Sealed-bid auction card: enter an Auction id + Your bid (e.g. 900) → "Place bid" (encrypted),
  then "Settle + pay".
- Say: "Liquidators bid on ciphertext; the winner is an encrypted running-max. Only the outcome
  is ever revealed — no front-running. A keeper can automate all of this."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 11 — Compliance (consent-based, scoped auditing)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Compliance".
- Step 1 (admin): paste an auditor address → "Set auditor". Confirm.
- Step 2 (borrower): pick cEUR→cUSDC, paste the same auditor address → "Grant scoped access". Confirm.
- Step 3 (auditor): pick the market + your borrower address → "Decrypt debt".
- Say: "The auditor decrypts EXACTLY one field. Any other handle → ACLNotAllowed. Compliance
  without a backdoor."

----------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 12 — Attack demo (why it all has to be encrypted) — strong closer
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Left menu → "Attack demo".
- Walk the rate-inversion story: if the borrow rate were public, an attacker inverts the curve to
  recover your hidden credit band — the leak defeats the encryption.
- Say: "That's why our rate stays an euint32 end-to-end. This page proves the attack, and proves
  our defense."

----------------------------------------------------------------
CLOSER
----------------------------------------------------------------
"Encrypted credit score, encrypted risk-priced rate, encrypted positions, confidential guarantors,
sealed-bid liquidations, scoped compliance — and the one thing no other confidential lender has:
borrow on reputation, below 100% collateral. That's the private credit layer for onchain lending."

Every sidebar page — one-line map:
  Dashboard     → submit encrypted inputs → private credit score + risk band  (Step 3)
  Markets       → 6 isolated markets, live Chainlink prices
  Borrow        → deposit + borrow (cEUR→cUSDC market)
  Positions     → decrypt your own debt/collateral + Repay
  Credit line   → decrypt reputation + credit line (killer feature)
  Guarantor     → confidential third-party backing
  Liquidations  → reveal-one-bit + sealed-bid auction
  Compliance    → scoped auditor decrypt
  Faucet        → mint encrypted tokens
  Attack demo   → rate-inversion proof

NOTE — two different "credit" things, don't mix them up:
  · Dashboard "score"  = from your submitted balance + age (Step 3). Decrypt with "Decrypt my score".
  · Credit line page   = from your repayment REPUTATION (grows after Step 7). "Decrypt my credit line".
